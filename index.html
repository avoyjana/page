Gaussian Functions and Quantum Mechanics

Gaussian Function

$$f(x) = e^{-x^2}$$

Two important uses:

$$\mathrm{erf}(x) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} \int_0^x e^{-z^2} dz$$
$$\delta(x) = \lim_{\epsilon \to 0} \eta_\epsilon(x), \quad \eta_\epsilon(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\epsilon} e^{-x^2/2\epsilon^2}$$

Heat/Diffusion kernel

Euler–Poisson Integral

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}$$

Normal Distribution

$$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}$$
$$\langle x \rangle = \mu, \quad \langle (x-\langle x\rangle)^2 \rangle = \sigma^2$$

Fourier Transform

\begin{align} \hat{f}(k) &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{ikx} f(x)\, dx \\ &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}} e^{-ikx} dx \end{align}

Let $x-\mu = t$:

\begin{align} &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} e^{-ik\mu} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\frac{t^2}{2\sigma^2}} e^{-ikt} dt \end{align}
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-ax^2 + bx} dx = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{a}} e^{\frac{b^2}{4a}}$$
$$\hat{f}(k) = e^{-ik\mu} e^{-\frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 k^2}$$

Moment Generating Function

$$M(t) = \mathbb{E}[e^{tx}] = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{tx} f(x) dx$$
$$M(t) = e^{\mu t + \frac{\sigma^2 t^2}{2}}$$
$$\mathbb{E}[x^n] = \left.\frac{d^n M}{dt^n}\right|_{t=0}$$
\begin{align} \mathbb{E}[x] &= \left.\frac{dM}{dt}\right|_{t=0} = \mu e^{\mu t + \frac{\sigma^2 t^2}{2}} + t\sigma^2 e^{\mu t + \frac{\sigma^2 t^2}{2}} \\ &= \mu \end{align}
$$\mathbb{E}[x^2] = \mu^2 + \sigma^2$$

Gaussian Wave Packet

A wave packet is a superposition of plane waves:

$$\Psi(x,0) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \phi(k) e^{ikx} dk$$

Take:

$$\phi(k) = \phi_0 e^{-\frac{(k-k_0)^2}{4\sigma_k^2}} e^{i(k_0-k)x_0}$$

Normalization:

$$\int |\phi(k)|^2 dk = 1$$
$$\phi_0 = \left(\frac{1}{2\pi\sigma_k^2}\right)^{1/4}$$

Compute $\Psi(x,0)$

Let $k-k_0 = t$

\begin{align} \Psi(x,0) &= \frac{\phi_0}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\frac{t^2}{4\sigma_k^2}} e^{-itx_0} e^{i(t+k_0)x} dt \\ &= \frac{\phi_0}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{ik_0 x} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\frac{t^2}{4\sigma_k^2}} e^{it(x-x_0)} dt \end{align}
\begin{equation} = \frac{\phi_0}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{ik_0 x} \sqrt{4\pi\sigma_k^2} e^{-\sigma_k^2 (x-x_0)^2} \end{equation}
\begin{equation} = A_0 e^{-\sigma_k^2 (x-x_0)^2} e^{ik_0 x} \end{equation}
\begin{equation} A_0 = \left(\frac{2\sigma_k^2}{\pi}\right)^{1/4} \end{equation}

Normalization in $x$-space

\begin{equation} \int |\Psi(x,0)|^2 dx = |A_0|^2 \int e^{-2\sigma_k^2 (x-x_0)^2} dx = 1 \end{equation}
\begin{equation} |A_0|^2 \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2\sigma_k^2}} = 1 \end{equation}

Symbolic Change

\begin{equation} p = \hbar k \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \sigma_p = \hbar \sigma_k \quad \Rightarrow \quad \sigma_k \sigma_x = \frac{1}{2} \end{equation}

Probability Distributions

\begin{equation} P(x) = |\Psi(x)|^2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma_x^2}} e^{-\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{2\sigma_x^2}} \end{equation}
\begin{equation} P(k) = |\phi(k)|^2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma_k^2}} e^{-\frac{(k-k_0)^2}{2\sigma_k^2}} \end{equation}

Uncertainty

\begin{equation} \langle x \rangle = x_0, \quad (\Delta x)^2 = \sigma_x^2 \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \langle k \rangle = k_0, \quad (\Delta k)^2 = \sigma_k^2 \end{equation}
\begin{equation} (\Delta x)^2 (\Delta p)^2 = \hbar^2 \sigma_x^2 \sigma_k^2 = \frac{\hbar^2}{4} \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \Delta x \Delta p = \frac{\hbar}{2} \end{equation}

Expectation Values from $\Psi(x)$

\begin{equation} \Psi(x,0) = \frac{1}{(2\pi\sigma_x^2)^{1/4}} e^{-\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{4\sigma_x^2}} e^{ik_0 x} \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x} = \left(ik_0 - \frac{x-x_0}{2\sigma_x^2}\right)\Psi \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \langle p \rangle = -i\hbar \int \Psi^* \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x} dx = \hbar k_0 \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \langle p^2 \rangle = \hbar^2 \left(k_0^2 + \frac{1}{4\sigma_x^2}\right) \end{equation}

Time Evolution

\begin{equation} H = \frac{p^2}{2m} = \frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m} \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \Psi(x,t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int \phi(k) e^{ikx} e^{-i\hbar k^2 t / 2m} dk \end{equation}

Define:

\begin{equation} a = \frac{1}{4\sigma_k^2} + \frac{i\hbar t}{2m} \end{equation}
\begin{equation} b = \frac{k_0}{2\sigma_k^2} + i(x-x_0) \end{equation}
\begin{equation} \int e^{-a k^2 + bk} dk = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{a}} e^{b^2 / 4a} \end{equation}

Final:

\begin{equation} \Psi(x,t) = \left(\frac{1}{2\pi\sigma_x^2}\right)^{1/4} \frac{\sigma_x}{\sigma_x(t)} \exp\left[-\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{4\sigma_x^2(t)} + ik_0 x \right] \end{equation}

Probability Density Evolution

\begin{equation} P(x,t) = |\Psi(x,t)|^2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma_x(t)} e^{-\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{2|\sigma_x(t)|^2}} \end{equation}

Special case:

\begin{equation} x_0 = 0, \quad k_0 = 0 \end{equation}
\begin{equation} P(x,t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma_x(t)} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2|\sigma_x(t)|^2}} \end{equation}
📐 Physics Note: These equations describe Gaussian functions and their fundamental role in quantum mechanics, particularly in wave packet dynamics, the uncertainty principle, and the time evolution of free particles.
